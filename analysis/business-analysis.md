# Sample Business Analysis

## Dataset
This analysis uses fictional data created for an educational demonstration. It does not represent actual company financial records.

## Methodology
- Gross profit per unit = selling price − estimated unit cost.
- Gross margin = gross profit per unit ÷ selling price × 100%.
- Estimated monthly gross profit = gross profit per unit × monthly sales volume.

## Results

| Product | Gross Profit per Unit (UZS) | Gross Margin | Estimated Monthly Gross Profit (UZS) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Concrete M200 | 100,000 | 22.2% | 12,000,000 |
| Concrete M300 | 120,000 | 22.6% | 10,200,000 |
| Paving tiles 60 mm | 27,000 | 28.4% | 24,300,000 |
| Wall block 20 cm | 6,500 | 31.0% | 9,100,000 |
| Garden curb | 4,300 | 34.4% | 4,730,000 |

**Total estimated monthly gross profit: UZS 60,330,000.**

## Key Findings
1. Garden curb has the highest sample gross margin at approximately 34.4%.
2. Paving tiles contribute the largest estimated monthly gross profit at UZS 24.3 million.
3. Concrete M200 and M300 have similar gross margins of approximately 22%.
4. Monthly gross profit depends on both the unit profit and the assumed sales volume.

## Recommendations
- Validate the cost and selling price of each product.
- Include payroll, utilities, repairs, depreciation, delivery, taxes, and other overhead before estimating net profit.
- Test how changes in cement prices and sales volumes affect profitability.

## Limitations
All inputs are fictional. Gross profit is not net profit, and the estimates exclude many operating expenses. The findings must not be treated as actual business results.
