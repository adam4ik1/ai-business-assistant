# AI Integration Workflow

## Project Goal
Demonstrate how AI can help analyze sample business data and turn the results into a clear presentation.

## Applications
- **ChatGPT:** Analyze data, calculate profitability, identify patterns, and generate recommendations.
- **GitHub:** Store the source dataset, project documentation, and analysis results.
- **Canva:** Create a presentation communicating the key findings.

## Workflow
1. Store the sample product dataset in GitHub.
2. Ask ChatGPT to calculate gross profit per unit, gross margin, and estimated monthly gross profit.
3. Review and document the analysis, including assumptions and limitations.
4. Transfer the findings into Canva to prepare a business presentation.
5. Capture screenshots showing the workflow and final results.

## Example AI Prompt
"Analyze the product dataset. Calculate gross profit per unit, gross margin percentage, and estimated monthly gross profit. Rank the products by gross margin and monthly gross profit. Clearly identify the data as fictional, explain excluded costs, and provide three practical recommendations."

## Limitations
All figures used in this classroom project are fictional examples. Gross profit is not net profit. Estimates may exclude overhead, payroll, utilities, repairs, depreciation, delivery, taxes, and rejected production.

Do not claim that an integration action succeeded unless it has been completed and verified.
